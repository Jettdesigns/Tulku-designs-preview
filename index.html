Not just a shop

We call it the Gallery, because every piece in here was chosen, not stocked.

Tulku Designs & Gifts sits on Gallery Walk, Tamborine Mountain — a warm, family-run shopfront of homewares, women's & men's fashion, children's gifts and a curated line of natural-fibre clothing carried down from Nepal.

Leather, linen, wool, silk and cotton, alongside brands like My Funky Bags, Millie and Mon, Marukanou, Love Lunamei and Olive Oil Skincare. Nothing mass-produced, nothing overpriced — just things worth having, at prices that still surprise people.

Family run

Junu, Andreas, and Apollo on the door.

Visitors keep telling us the same thing: it's the welcome that brings them back as much as the shelves. That's not by accident — this is a small, family-run gallery, and it's usually Junu or Andreas you'll meet when you walk in, not staff working a shift.

We buy for the shop the way we'd buy for our own home — natural materials, honest prices, and nothing we wouldn't want to own ourselves. If we don't have quite the right gift, we'll happily point you toward something that will be.

Apollo greets everyone at the door. He's become something of a local fixture on Gallery Walk — regulars stop to say hello to him as much as to us.

Sourced, not sourced-out

Every natural-fibre piece in the Nepal Collection is chosen in person, not ordered from a catalogue.
